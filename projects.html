Overview

A lead pipeline I built for Lapiz Blue, a construction chemicals distributor in Dubai. It gathers incoming leads in one place, lets the sales team check them, and sends clean leads straight into Zoho CRM.

The problem

Leads arrived through different channels and were typed into the CRM by hand, which was slow and easy to get wrong.

What it does

  1. 01

    Pulls new leads from the counter form and the leads email inbox with one Fetch Leads button

  2. 02

    AI reads free text enquiries and pre-fills the lead details

  3. 03

    The team reviews each lead as an editable draft: company, salesman, source, quantity, category and products

  4. 04

    Clean leads go to Zoho CRM in one click, assigned to the next salesman in turn
